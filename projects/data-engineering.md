# Data Engineering

Projects covering data pipelines, geospatial ETL, and modern data stack tools.

## [Detecting Methane Anomalies in Alberta with Satellite Data](../posts/emissions-ghg.md)

A medallion pipeline (DuckDB · Iceberg · Airflow · dbt) that cross-references Sentinel-5P satellite observations with Alberta Energy Regulator facility declarations to flag statistical anomalies — 340 flags detected across 73,908 facility-month pairs.
