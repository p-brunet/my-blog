# Data Sources

A curated list of open data sources used across projects on this blog.

---

## Geospatial

- [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) — collaborative worldwide base map
- [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) — free vector and raster map data
- [Copernicus DEM](https://www.copernicus.eu/en) — European digital elevation models
- [SRTM](https://www2.jpl.nasa.gov/srtm/) — NASA 30m global elevation data

## LiDAR & Elevation

- [VCGI (Vermont)](https://geodata.vermont.gov/) — high-resolution LiDAR tiles (35–70 cm)
- [IGN (France)](https://geoservices.ign.fr/) — French national geographic data
- [USGS 3DEP](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program) — US national LiDAR program

---

## Energy

### Infrastructure

- [ENTSO-E Transparency Platform](https://transparency.entsoe.eu/) — European electricity transmission data (generation, load, cross-border flows)
- [OpenStreetMap Power](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Power) — mapped power lines, substations and plants
- [Global Power Plant Database](https://datasets.wri.org/dataset/globalpowerplantdatabase) — World Resources Institute worldwide plant inventory

### Renewables

- [Global Solar Atlas](https://globalsolaratlas.info/) — solar irradiance and PV potential
- [Global Wind Atlas](https://globalwindatlas.info/) — wind speed and capacity factor maps
- [IRENA](https://www.irena.org/Data) — International Renewable Energy Agency statistics

## Mobility

- [Circuit Électrique (Hydro-Québec)](https://www.hydroquebec.com/electric-vehicles/charging-stations/) — EV charging network in Québec
- [transport.data.gouv.fr](https://transport.data.gouv.fr/) — GTFS and mobility data for France
- [OpenMobilityData](https://transitfeeds.com/) — worldwide GTFS feeds
- [OSM Charging Network](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:amenity%3Dcharging_station) — mapped EV charging stations
