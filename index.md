I am a data engineer / scientist (applied math and computer science). Over the years I found my interest in energy and geospatial data. I blend these two using my skills in modeling and engineering. Beyond my profession, I am a passionnate cyclist and urban spaces lover; I might also include few posts on  mobility in this blog.

I enjoy tackling complex challenges with clear objectives for everyone: optimizing a physical model or modeling wave propagation through neural networks. I am passionate about working with various types of data, particularly geo-spatial data. I am always eager to learn new methods and tools to keep my skills in data engineering and data science up-to-date. You'll find my professional experience and academic background on my [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/brunet-paul1/?locale=en-US).

**The purpose of this blog:**

This space aims to present and share findings, skills and workaround with data applied with real world constraints. If any of this resonates with you, whether you want to collaborate, share feedback, or just talk data, don't hesitate to reach out. I'm always happy to connect with people who are curious about the same things.

**A few things I've built:**

- Modeled radio wave propagation with deep learning (CNN) to replace traditional physical simulations
- Developed a hybrid ML + optimization model for European electricity price forecasting
- Collected data to compare hydroelectric power pricing mechanisms around the world
- Designed ML models for industrial load forecasting and climate scenario analysis
- Developed ML models to help industrial planner to assess future workload
- Had a lot of fun to generate poster-quality topographic maps from open LiDAR data ([JayPeakMap](posts/jay-peak-map))
- Created dashboards and automated reporting systems for operational data
